Mr & Mrs Reyes Condominium

This luxury condominium layout embraces a Modern Contemporary Elegance design concept that seamlessly blends opulent materiality with precise spatial geometry across an expansive, open-plan floor plate. The design language is underpinned by a crisp, continuous base of white-and-gray veined marble tiling, which bounces natural light and unifies the interconnected living, dining, and culinary zones. Spatial hierarchy is masterfully established overhead through sophisticated, multi-tiered drop ceilings accented with warm, integrated cove lighting and architectural black trim lines that frame minimalist, geometric lighting fixtures. The material palette expertly balances luxury and organic warmth, contrasting highly figured gray marble feature walls, fluted timber paneling, and rich walnut-toned cabinetry against brushed gold accents and plush, neutral-toned upholstery. From the bespoke marble dining island to the sprawling master suites equipped with integrated shelving, sliding pocket doors, and a spa-like marble bathroom featuring a freestanding vessel sink, every element is tailored to create a fluid, sophisticated residential sanctuary that feels both grandly theatrical and intensely comforting.
