MS BRIONES 3-STOREY RESIDENCE

This modern three-storey residential concept seamlessly orchestrates a sophisticated, high-contrast dialogue between its exterior architectural form and its bright, voluminous interior spaces. Externally, the building presents a striking contemporary facade composed of interlocking geometric volumes where crisp white masonry interfaces with warm wood paneling, rugged textured stone columns, and sharp charcoal-toned framing elements. The residence prioritizes privacy and security at the street level with an integrated, wood-and-iron sliding gate system and high perimeter walls, while dynamically opening up on the upper levels through expansive, floor-to-ceiling glass windows and structural glass balconies that overlook the surrounding neighborhood. Inside, the home transitions into an airy, double-height open-concept layout that masterfully balances clean, minimalist modernism with cozy, organic textures. The ground floor integrates the living, dining, and kitchen areas, unified by rich, multi-toned hardwood flooring and a neutral color palette. This public zone features a gourmet kitchen with sleek white cabinetry, a central island with wood accents, a striking brick-textured accent wall, and an adjacent high-ceiling dining space lit by contemporary pendant fixtures. A floating staircase with a minimalist glass balustrade leads to the private quarters on the upper levels, where the design language culminates in a luxurious master bedroom. This private sanctuary mirrors the home's core aesthetic, showcasing a custom marble-and-light-slatted TV backdrop, sophisticated warm cove lighting within a recessed ceiling, an elegant floating console, and private access to an outdoor balcony via expansive sliding glass doors, offering an immersive indoor-outdoor living experience.
