Mr & Mrs Sira Extension Residence

This house extension follows a modern, minimalist design concept that emphasizes clean lines, functional spaces, and visual openness. The exterior features a balanced combination of neutral tones, flat rooflines, and vertical elements that add depth and character, while the gated frontage ensures privacy and security. Inside, the extension creates a seamless flow between living areas, highlighted by warm lighting, built-in storage, and contemporary finishes such as wood accents and marble textures. full glass windows and open layouts enhance natural light and ventilation, resulting in a comfortable, stylish, and highly livable environment tailored for modern family living. Additionally, the design thoughtfully adapts to its surroundings by aligning with the architectural language of neighboring homes—using similar proportions, materials, and color palettes—to maintain visual harmony within the community. This approach reinforces a cohesive streetscape while still allowing the house to express a refined, contemporary identity.
